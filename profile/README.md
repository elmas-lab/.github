# Elmas Lab

### Reliable AI and computational methods for biomedical discovery

The **Elmas Lab** develops interpretable and uncertainty-aware computational methods for biomedical research. We combine electrical and electronics engineering, signal processing, machine learning, optimization, computer vision, and multi-omics data science.

🏛️ **Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering**  
[Necmettin Erbakan University](https://erbakan.edu.tr/tr/akademik-personel/7998-abdulkadir-elmas)

> We solve inverse problems in biomedical data: inferring hidden biological and clinical states from noisy, incomplete, and indirect measurements—while quantifying uncertainty and enabling reproducible research.

## Research areas

- 🧬 **Population-aware genomic AI**  
  Genetic-variant interpretation, Bayesian evidence integration, calibration, and uncertainty-aware prediction.

- 🔬 **AI for digital pathology and proteomics**  
  Whole-slide imaging, pathology foundation models, image–omics integration, and molecular target prediction.

- 🗺️ **Spatial omics and inverse problems**  
  Sparse recovery, constrained optimization, spatial regularization, and cell-type deconvolution.

- 🎯 **AI for precision oncology**  
  Cancer proteogenomics, biomarker discovery, post-transcriptional regulation, and treatment-response prediction.
